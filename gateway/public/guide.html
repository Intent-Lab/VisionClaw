VisionClaw Participant Guide

Thanks for joining the study. This page gets the app on your phone and shows what you can do with it. Setup takes about ten minutes with a researcher nearby.

1. Install the app

Open the install page
  • iPhone: the researcher registers your phone first. Then open the install page in Safari, tap Install for iPhone, close Safari, and wait for the icon on your home screen.
  • Android: tap Download for Android, open the downloaded file, and allow installs from your browser when asked.

2. Sign in with Google

The first screen has one button: Sign in with Google. Tap it, choose your own Google account, and approve the permissions. Signing in creates your study account and connects your Google Calendar in the same step, so there is nothing to type and no code to keep.

If the browser says the sign-in expired, come back to the app and tap the button again. You never need an access code; that option only exists for people running their own server.

3. Connected apps

Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Connected Apps. Google Calendar is already connected from sign-in. The others are optional; connect the ones you would actually use:

  • Gmail: the researcher adds your Gmail address to a test list first, then tap Connect. Google expires this connection every 7 days; the app tells you when to reconnect from the same screen.
  • Notion: tap Connect and pick which pages the assistant may use.
  • Slack: only for members of the study's Slack workspace.

Each connection is used only when you ask, and you can disconnect any of them from this screen.

4. Your first call

Tap the call button. A small label near the top shows the assistant's state (listening, thinking, speaking). Point the camera at something and just talk:

  • "What is this?"
  • "What's the weather here today?"
  • "Add this to my shopping list."

Captions of what you and the assistant said appear at the bottom. Cards with details appear near the top; swipe them away when you are done.

5. Glasses day

  1. Pair your Ray-Ban Meta glasses with the Meta AI app on your phone.
  2. In the Meta AI app, open its settings and turn on Developer Mode. Without it the glasses refuse to link to VisionClaw.
  3. In VisionClaw, open Settings and set Camera source to Glasses. Put the glasses on; the call starts by itself.

"Waiting for glasses video" means the glasses are asleep or folded; wearing them wakes the link. A short stall when the video starts is normal, and the app reconnects on its own if the link drops. If it stays stuck, turn the glasses off and on.

6. Things to try

Ask about what you see"Read this label." "Which of these is cheaper?"
Look something up"When does this place close?" "What's the score of the game?"
Remember things"Remember this." "Add eggs to my shopping list." "What's on my list?" "Remove the eggs."
Your calendar"What's on my calendar today?" "Add lunch with Sam on Friday at noon."
Bigger tasks"Find me three reviews of this." "Compare these two products." The assistant works on these in the background and tells you when it is done, sometimes at the start of your next call.
Email (if connected)"Summarize my unread email." "Email this to Sam."

Not supported in this study: buying things, logging into websites, controlling smart-home devices, Slack, Notion, and text messages. If you find yourself wanting one of these, tell the researcher; that is useful to us.

7. What happens to my data

  • Your voice is transcribed. The transcripts, the assistant's replies, and the actions it took are logged to your study account and reviewed by the research team.
  • Camera video is used live to answer you and is not stored by the study. For some tasks a single frame is sent to the assistant's action system and discarded afterward.
  • Notes you ask the assistant to save are stored as text until you delete them. Avoid saving anything you would not want the research team to read.
  • Connected accounts are used only when you ask. You can disconnect them any time from Settings or from your Google account page.
  • In the study data your account appears under a random id, not your name or email.

8. If something goes wrong

"Waiting for agent" does not changeThe assistant service is down. Tell the researcher.
Sign-in expired or failedReturn to the app and tap Sign in with Google again.
Gmail says access blockedYour address is not on the test list yet. Tell the researcher.
"Error opening link" with glassesTurn on Developer Mode in the Meta AI app and try again.
The assistant said it was working on something, then the call endedThe answer arrives at the start of your next call.
